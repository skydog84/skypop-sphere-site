2. Promotion period

Each weekly tournament runs Monday 00:00 UTC through the following Monday 00:00 UTC ("Entry Period"), beginning the first Monday after the feature is enabled in a released build, and continuing until terminated by Sponsor with reasonable notice in the app.

3. Eligibility

Open to natural persons who are (a) at least 18 years old (or the age of majority in their jurisdiction, whichever is greater) at time of entry, and (b) legal residents of an eligible jurisdiction. NOT eligible: residents of New York, Florida and Rhode Island; Quebec; Italy; Brazil; Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria and Russia; employees of Sponsor and their household members; and any jurisdiction where this promotion would be unlawful. This list is kept in sync with assets/config/sweepstakes_exclusions.json; the app blocks sweepstakes UI in excluded regions. Compliance with local law is the entrant's responsibility.

4. How to enter — two free methods

(a) Gameplay entry (free): during the Entry Period, play the Weekly tournament in SkyPop Sphere (free download, free entry; the rewarded-ad and remove-ads purchase options do NOT affect tournament scoring or entry). Your highest valid score on the week's seeded Rush sphere is your entry.

(b) Alternate free entry (no app required): email skydog8426@gmail.com during the Entry Period with subject "SkyPop Weekly Entry", your full name, age confirmation (18+), and country/state of residence. One alternate entry per person per Entry Period. Alternate entries are assigned a score equal to the median of all gameplay entries for that week.

5. Winner determination — skill-based ranking

This is a skill contest: every participant plays the IDENTICAL, deterministically-generated sphere (same seed), for the same fixed 90-second duration, and the highest score wins. No random drawing determines the winner among gameplay entries. Sponsor's anti-cheat validation (score bounds, replay-hash verification, re-simulation) is final; entries that fail validation are void.

6. Prizes — the top ten

Ten (10) prizes per Entry Period, awarded by final leaderboard rank, paid as digital gift cards:

| Rank | Prize | ARV | |---|---|---| | 1st | $25 digital gift card | $25 | | 2nd | $15 digital gift card | $15 | | 3rd | $10 digital gift card | $10 | | 4th – 10th | $5 digital gift card each | $35 | | Total per Entry Period | | $85 |

Total ARV of all prizes in any calendar year: $4,420 (52 Entry Periods × $85). This figure is deliberately held under the ~$5,000 aggregate threshold at which several states require promotion registration and bonding. If Sponsor raises prize values, the annual total must be recalculated and registration reconsidered before the change takes effect.

Prizes are non-transferable; no substitution except by Sponsor. All taxes are the winner's responsibility. Sponsor will issue any tax forms required by law.

7. Winner notification & claim

Provisional winners are shown on the in-app leaderboard at the end of the Entry Period and must claim by emailing skydog8426@gmail.com from any address within fourteen (14) days, providing their leaderboard player ID and proof of eligibility (age and residence). Failure to claim, or failure of verification, forfeits the prize, which passes to the next-ranked eligible entrant. Prize fulfillment occurs entirely outside the app.

8. General conditions

Sponsor may disqualify any entry obtained by cheating, automation, exploit, or multiple accounts. Sponsor may cancel or modify the promotion for integrity or legal reasons, awarding prizes for completed periods. By entering, entrants release Apple Inc. and Google LLC from all claims arising from this promotion and agree these Official Rules govern.

9. Privacy

Entry data is processed per the SkyPop Sphere Privacy Policy (https://skydog84.github.io/skypop-sphere-site/privacy-policy.html). Winner claim emails are used solely for prize fulfillment and legal compliance.
