SkyPop Sphere

A planet spins inside a shell of metal satellites. Spin it with one finger, tap matching alloys to pop them, chain a Depth Strike straight through the layers, and burst the core before the clock runs out.

Three ways to play

  • Journey — sixty seconds a world, climbing Earth to Moon.
  • Zen — no score, no fail, no ads. Just the sphere.
  • Rush — ninety seconds, seeded daily and weekly tournaments where everyone plays the identical board.

Free to play. An optional one-time purchase removes ads.
